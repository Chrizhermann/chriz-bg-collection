# Chriz Easy IWD 0.1.14

App **0.1.14** bundles collection **0.1.15**. Chris's Recommended includes **143 default
components across 34 install runs and 33 mods**, with **18 recruitable companions**.

## Highlights

- **Let a party member qualify for a dialogue option.** Chriz IWD Party Dialog
  **v0.1.0** checks the class, kit and race of living party members in the current
  area during world and quest conversations. A qualifying party member can speak and
  the dialogue uses that character's details. Personal companion conversations still
  address your main character. The mod is on by default with EEex under **Quality of life**
  in **Customize**.
- **Round Clock counts time spent travelling and resting.** BG Rebalance **v0.8.12**
  (component **361**) counts travel, rest and scripted advances of the game clock toward
  the wait after casting a spell, using an item, swinging a weapon or trying to hide,
  and toward the next Contingency check. You can click Stealth while paused once Hide
  in Shadows is ready. Round Clock is on by default with EEex under **Spells** in
  **Customize**.
- **BuffBot v1.16.2 updates the Italian translation.** Get it for an existing game from
  **Updates → BuffBot**.

## Testing and limits

- Chriz IWD Party Dialog passed 159 offline checks. An IWD:EE 2.7.3.0 quiet lab
  verified that party members could unlock dialogue options and take over as speaker,
  and that personal companion conversations still addressed the main character.
- Round Clock's paused Stealth behavior was verified in BG2:EE and IWD:EE 2.7.3.0
  quiet labs. A full combined installation and a full IWD campaign playthrough are
  still pending for this release.

## Installing and updating

- On app **0.1.7–0.1.13**, choose **Updates → Update Chriz Easy IWD app**.
- On **0.1.1–0.1.6** or for a first-time setup, download and run
  [Chriz.Easy.IWD_0.1.14_x64-setup.exe](https://github.com/Chrizhermann/chriz-easy-iwd/releases/download/v0.1.14/Chriz.Easy.IWD_0.1.14_x64-setup.exe)
  from the [v0.1.14 release page](https://github.com/Chrizhermann/chriz-easy-iwd/releases/tag/v0.1.14).
- Party Dialog and Round Clock come with a new installation. Choose **New installation**
  to use them for your next playthrough.

Tested with Icewind Dale: Enhanced Edition 2.7.3.0 on 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 (about 8 GB of free space); other usable Windows copies install as Less tested.
