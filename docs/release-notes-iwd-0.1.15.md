# Chriz Easy IWD 0.1.15

App **0.1.15** (bundled collection **0.1.15**, **33 mods with 143 components** and
**18 companions**) adds Round Clock and party-wide dialogue checks to new installations.
Both are on by default with EEex in the recommended setup.

## Highlights

- **Round Clock** counts time spent travelling, resting or passing time in a script
  toward the wait after a spell, item use, weapon swing or attempt to hide, and toward
  the next contingency check. You can click **Stealth** while paused as soon as Hide in
  Shadows is ready. This is component 361 of BG Rebalance v0.8.12.
- **Party-wide class, kit, and race checks in world dialogues.** Chriz IWD Party Dialog
  v0.1.0 lets any living party member in the same area qualify for a reply in a world
  or quest conversation. That character can step forward and speak the line under
  their own name. Personal companion conversations use the protagonist.
- **BuffBot v1.16.2** refreshes 72 of the 252 entries in its Italian catalog, including
  spell names and option labels. For a game in progress, close it, open **Updates**,
  choose **Check for a BuffBot update**, and then choose **Update BuffBot**.

Separate IWD:EE 2.7.3.0 labs verified using Stealth while paused and a qualifying
party member taking over a conversation. The combined 143-component installation
still awaits a full test run.

On app **0.1.7–0.1.13**, use **Updates → Update Chriz Easy IWD app**. On
**0.1.1–0.1.6**, install `Chriz.Easy.IWD_0.1.15_x64-setup.exe` by hand once.
Choose **New installation** for Round Clock and the new dialogue options.

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), Icewind Dale: Enhanced Edition (tested on **2.7.3.0**, which is
required for the EEex features) and about 8 GB of free space are required. Keep the
computer awake during installation.
