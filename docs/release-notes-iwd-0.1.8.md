# Chriz Easy IWD 0.1.8

**A fix for the companion-banter crash.** The Icewind Dale Crossmod Banter Pack could
pick a dead party member and crash the game. Banter now starts only when both partners
are alive and in the same area. Its chances and timers stay the same.

Already playing? Update the app, close the game, then open **Updates** and choose
**Check for a hotpatch** and **Apply hotpatch**. The fix works with current saves in
eligible installations made by earlier releases. If the earlier fight update is still
pending, apply it first; the banter fix is offered next. New installations include the fix.

**Arszyk's Wonderful Decay** joins the recommended setup: Lava's vampire fighter/druid
and a late-game quest that also works without him. This mod is a beta. It is included
only in new installations, and can be left out in **Customize**. Existing games keep
their current companion selection.

The recommended setup now contains **29 mods with 139 components** and **18 companions**.
Chriz IWD Encounters is pinned to 0.2.1. BuffBot remains pinned to 1.12.6 for new
installations; the separate **Update BuffBot** action introduced in app 0.1.7 can bring
an existing game to its newest release.

On app **0.1.7**, use **Updates → Update Chriz Easy IWD app**. On **0.1.1–0.1.6**, install
the new setup by hand once; subsequent app updates are available inside the app.
Updating the app leaves installed games and saves unchanged. A game hotpatch is applied
only when selected separately.

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a clean Icewind Dale: Enhanced Edition 2.7.3.0 and about 8 GB
of free space are required. The tested game source is the English Steam version.
Keep the computer awake during installation. This remains an early release, and not
every optional combination or the complete Arszyk quest has been playtested.

The setup has a Tauri updater signature, not a Windows Authenticode publisher signature.
Windows may still show an unknown-publisher warning. Download from the official release
and compare its published SHA-256 checksum.
