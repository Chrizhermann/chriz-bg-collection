# Chriz Easy IWD 0.1.13

**Spoken overhead text stays visible above Tab nameplates and HP Over Head.** App **0.1.13**
(bundled collection **0.1.13**, **31 mods with 141 components** and **18 companions**) adds
**Chriz Overhead Text v0.1.0** by default (with EEex). Updating the app leaves installed games
and saves unchanged; new installations get the new mod.

## Highlights

- **Spoken lines stack cleanly above nameplates and overhead HP.** Holding or toggling **Tab /
  Reveal Details** or enabling **`HP Over Head`** no longer hides spoken overhead lines
  (`DisplayStringHead`, `VerbalConstant` subtitles). Nameplates and overhead HP use their own
  slot while spoken text stacks `4 px` above them, scaled with area zoom.
- **On by default, optional in Customize.** Enabled in the recommended setup (requires EEex)
  and switchable under **Interface** in **Customize**.

On app **0.1.7** to **0.1.12**, use **Updates → Update Chriz Easy IWD app**. On
**0.1.1–0.1.6**, install `Chriz.Easy.IWD_0.1.13_x64-setup.exe` by hand once.

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), Icewind Dale: Enhanced Edition 2.7.3.0 and about 8 GB of free
space are required. Keep the computer awake during installation.
