# HQ SoundClips for BG2EE — community opinion brief (2026-08-21)

Requested by Chris (worksheet: "have a subagent check for people's opinion").

- **What it does:** restores high-quality voice audio for BG2EE-added content that Beamdog
  downgraded from patch v1.3 on — EE NPCs (Rasaad, Dorn, Neera, Hexxat) and Black Pits II.
  Clips sourced from the original v1.2 files, optimized (mono, 24 kHz) to ~40 % size:
  7,659 files, ~197 MB download. Not an NPC voice pack — it's audio restoration.
- **Opinion:** consistently positive / "no-brainer" (Beamdog thread 61849: pre-mod EE-NPC
  audio called "tinny, muffled"). No quality complaints, no install-order constraints
  (installs fine after EET), no reported mod conflicts.
- **Maintenance:** Argent77, latest v1.3 (PI labels, pt-BR/Polish translations); BG2EE +
  EET supported; distributed as .iemod/zip. No explicit 2.7 statement found (G3 thread
  403'd) — the underlying Beamdog downgrade was never fixed upstream, so it stays relevant.
