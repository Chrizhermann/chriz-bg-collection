# Chriz Easy IWD 0.1.1 — first public Windows release

A few-click installer for ChrizFader's curated Icewind Dale: Enhanced Edition setup, the one
played on stream. It copies your clean IWD:EE 2.7.3.0 into a new folder and installs the
recommended mods there; your original game stays unchanged.

- **28 recommended mods, 126 components**, each downloaded from its official source and checked
  against the tested version. Customize adds an interface (Lefreut's Enhanced UI or
  Infinity UI++), eight quest mods by Lava and many smaller options.
- **Smarter fights:** Sword Coast Stratagems, plus Chriz IWD Encounters for the IWD:EE casters
  SCS misses and for enemy priests who prepare when they see your party.
- **17 new companions** with crossmod banter, Unfinished Business, the Tweaks Anthology,
  Hidden Gameplay Options, the Icewind Dale Graphical Overhaul, Bubb's Spell Menu and BuffBot.
- **Hotpatches:** later fight updates can be applied from **Updates → Hotpatch** to an
  installation that stays close to the recommended setup, so you can keep your current run.
- **Pause and resume**, optional manual-mod pauses for experienced modders, and the optional
  BG Radar Overlay.

See the [README](https://github.com/Chrizhermann/chriz-easy-iwd#readme) for the full list of
changes, every mod with its author and version, and the known limitations.

You need Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a clean Icewind Dale: Enhanced Edition 2.7.3.0 (tested
with the English Steam version) and about 8 GB of free space. Keep the computer awake during
installation.

This is an early release. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows may warn about an
unknown publisher; compare the SHA-256 checksum below before running it.
