# Chriz Easy IWD 0.1.2

**Marketh's Ring** from Unfinished Business is now off by default. You can still turn it on in
Customize. A player reported that with it, Marketh can vanish together with his signet, so the
quest cannot be finished.

Nothing else changes: the same 28 recommended mods, now with 125 components. Installations made
with 0.1.1 keep working and can still take hotpatches, so there is no need to reinstall.

See the [README](https://github.com/Chrizhermann/chriz-easy-iwd#readme) for everything the setup
contains, every mod with its author and version, and the known limitations.

You need Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a clean Icewind Dale: Enhanced Edition 2.7.3.0 (tested
with the English Steam version) and about 8 GB of free space. Keep the computer awake during
installation.

This is an early release. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows may warn about an
unknown publisher; compare the SHA-256 checksum below before running it.
