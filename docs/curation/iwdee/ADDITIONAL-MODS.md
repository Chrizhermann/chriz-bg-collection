automatically enabled add-on list. The user plans to examine companions further.

## Intake fields for each new candidate

- Exact mod identity, author, maintained source and candidate release.
- IWDEE and HoW/TotL support, languages, acquisition route and redistribution limits.
- Spoiler-free scope: approximate content size, gameplay role, dialogue/voice coverage.
- New kits, spells, items, rewards and effects on progression or difficulty.
- Overlap/dependencies with chosen NPCs, Artisan's, Bardic Wonders, SCS and UI mods.
- User's mod-level decision; then source-derived component menu and individual choices.
- Remaining compatibility checks and independent installation/gameplay evidence.

User-facing summaries must omit recruitment locations, quest solutions, rewards,
enemy identities and plot events. Assess suspected power creep from the actual mod
changes rather than treating either the concern or an author's balance claim as proof.
